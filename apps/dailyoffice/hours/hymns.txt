[ISTE_CONFESSOR]
TITLE=ISTE CONFESSOR
V=The Lord loved him, and adorned him.
R=He clothed him with a robe of glory.
This the Confessor of the Lord, whose triumph
Now all the people celebrate with gladness,
On this his feast day stood serene, victorious,
Reigning in heaven.

Now holy Church his heavenly crown proclaiming,
Sings of his virtue, purity and valour,
Bearing glad witness to the faith and courage
He showed before men.

Glory and honour, virtue, strength and conquest,
Be unto thee, O Father, Son and Spirit,
One God eternal in the highest heaven,
World without end.
[END]

[DEUS_TUORUM_MILITUM]
TITLE=DEUS TUORUM MILITUM
V=Thou hast crowned him with glory and worship, O Lord.
R=And hast made him to have dominion of the works of thy hands.
O God, of those that fought thy fight,
Whose warrior vanquished in the strife,
From every sin deliver us,
And grant us now eternal life.

O grant that we, like him faithful,
May fight the fight and keep the faith,
And win the victor's crown of life
Through triumph over sin and death.
[END]

[ECCE_JAM_NOCTIS]
TITLE=ECCE JAM NOCTIS
V=The Lord is King, and hath put on glorious apparel.
R=The Lord hath put on his apparel, and girded himself with strength.
(Translation by W. J. Courthope)
Lo, the dim shadows of the night are waning;
Radiantly glowing, dawn of day returneth;
Fervent in spirit, to the mighty Maker
Humbly make we our prayer.

So may our God, his boundless mercy showing,
Banish all sorrow, pardon all our weakness,
Granting us heaven, where in bliss eternal
Saints ever praise him.

Praise be to Father, praise to Son be given,
And to thee, Spirit, heavenly Comforter,
Blessed and glorious Trinity undivided,
Now and for ever. Amen.
[END]

[LUCIS_CREATOR_OPTIME]
TITLE=LUCIS CREATOR OPTIME
V=Lord, let my prayer be set forth in thy sight.
R=As the incense.
O blest Creator of the light,
Who mak'st the day with splendour bright,
And framing all at first for man,
Didst with the light the world began.

O Father, that we ask be done,
Through Jesus Christ, thine only Son;
Who, with the Holy Ghost and thee,
Doth live and reign eternally. Amen.
[END]
